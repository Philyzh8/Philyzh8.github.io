

## Biography

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Jilin University. I received my B.Sc. degree in Mathematics from Jilin University in 2020 and subsequently joined the School of Artificial Intelligence for postgraduate study. In 2022, I continued my doctoral study through the master's-to-doctoral program.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **bioinformatics**, **artificial intelligence**, and **computational biology**. I focus on developing deep learning and graph-based computational methods for analyzing high-dimensional biological data, particularly single-cell and spatial omics data.

My current research aims to characterize cellular heterogeneity, tissue spatial organization, developmental dynamics, and gene regulatory mechanisms from large-scale multi-omics datasets. I am particularly interested in integrating biological knowledge with artificial intelligence models to improve the accuracy, interpretability, and generalizability of computational methods for complex biological systems.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **AI for Science**, with particular emphasis on the development of computational methods for understanding complex biological systems.

### Bioinformatics

Development of computational and statistical methods for analyzing large-scale genomic, transcriptomic, epigenomic, and multi-omics datasets.

### Deep Learning for Biological Data

Design of deep learning models, including graph neural networks, contrastive learning, generative models, and representation learning methods, for extracting meaningful biological patterns from high-dimensional and sparse data.

### Single-cell Omics

Analysis of single-cell transcriptomic and epigenomic data to characterize cellular heterogeneity, identify cell populations, infer developmental trajectories, and investigate gene regulatory mechanisms.

### Spatial Omics

Development of computational methods for spatial transcriptomics, spatial epigenomics, and spatial multi-omics data, including:

* Spatial domain identification
* Spatially variable feature detection
* Cell-type deconvolution and annotation
* Multi-slice integration and alignment
* Three-dimensional tissue reconstruction
* Spatial gene regulation analysis

### Spatiotemporal Biological Data Integration

Integration of biological samples collected across spatial locations, developmental stages, or temporal conditions to reconstruct dynamic tissue organization and cellular transitions.

### Computational Gene Regulation

Investigation of gene regulatory networks, chromatin accessibility, transcriptional regulation, and cell–cell communication using single-cell and spatial multi-omics data.

## Research Vision

My long-term goal is to develop biologically informed and interpretable artificial intelligence methods that transform large-scale single-cell and spatial omics data into meaningful knowledge about cellular identity, tissue organization, developmental processes, and disease mechanisms.

I am also interested in interdisciplinary collaborations involving bioinformatics, artificial intelligence, genomics, spatial biology, and precision medicine.
